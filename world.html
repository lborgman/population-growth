World population growth

Assume that:
  • Population on our planet as a whole is declining.
  • Population in one single country grows very fast.

What does that mean for future number of humans? It may surprize you that it can lead to that the human population on our planet grows very fast.

How can that happen?

It is all in the math. Growth in the long term is exponential.

Think of doubling something. Compare that with halving.

Look at about absolute sizes. Doubling will grow very quickly. But halving does not change that much.

We can use that simple fact to roughly estimate population growth under our assumption. I hope this page can help you think about it.

Note: Here we only look at long term growth. In the near time population "pyramid" has a big effect. However we do not have sufficient data for the "pyramids" to include that. And after a generation the exponential growth is all that matters. (If you want a closer look at near term growth you can look at World Population History.)
